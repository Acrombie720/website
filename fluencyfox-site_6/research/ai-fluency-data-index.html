How this index works

Five rules, applied to everything on this page.

  • Published figures only. Every number here comes from a named, publicly available source. Nothing is modelled, extrapolated or estimated by us.
  • Sample size and date attached to every figure. A 74% from 1,000 hiring managers in June 2026 is a different object from a 74% from an undisclosed sample, and you should be able to see which you are looking at.
  • Secondary sources are labelled as secondary. Where we could not reach the primary report, the entry says which publication we took it from.
  • No figure is adjusted. Rounding and phrasing follow the original. Where a publisher gives a split, we show the split.
  • Corrections welcome. If we have misread your data or you have newer figures, tell us and we will fix it in the next monthly pass.

Figures marked sample not disclosed are included because they are widely cited, not because we consider them equivalent in quality to the surveyed studies. Treat them accordingly.

Methodology

Sources
  • Nine named publications: TestGorilla, ZipRecruiter Economic Research, iCIMS Insights, Study.com, ManpowerGroup, Anthropic, CompTIA, Ashby, and IDC and Lightcast via secondary reporting.
  • Every figure links to the publication it came from. Full list in the Sources section above.
Sample size
49 figures across 9 sources. Underlying samples range from 1,000 respondents to 39,063 employers across 41 countries; three sources do not disclose a sample.
Date range
Fieldwork from July 2025 to September 2026. Compiled 14 September 2026.
Limitations
  • This is a compilation, not original research. Fluencyfox collected and labelled these figures; it did not produce any of them.
  • Figures marked sample not disclosed are included because they are widely cited, not because they are equivalent in quality to the surveyed studies.
  • Two entries come from secondary reporting, where the primary report was not publicly reachable. Those entries name the publication they were taken from.
  • Definitions of “AI fluency” differ between sources. Figures are not directly comparable across publishers, and are not combined or averaged anywhere on this page.
  • Self-reported survey data throughout. None of these figures observe what people actually do.
