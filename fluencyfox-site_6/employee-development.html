WHAT YOU DO WITH IT
01

Point training at the real gaps

Stop buying one course for everyone. The map shows you which capability areas are weak and which teams are already fine, so the budget goes where it moves something.

02

Find your internal champions

Every organisation already has people quietly doing this well, and they are rarely the ones you would guess. Rollouts led by them land better than rollouts led by a vendor.
