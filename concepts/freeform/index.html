Scoring

Three scores. Your weights.

Each score is out of 10, and you choose how much each one counts when the test is set up. Try other weights on these example candidates and watch the order change.

Solution
What they shipped: correctness, completeness and polish of the deliverable, judged against the objective you set.
Communication
How clearly they explain what they are doing and why. Every word they say is transcribed, so you are sitting in on the work.
AI collaboration
How they work with Maya, the AI stakeholder: what they ask her, and how they check and use her answers. Their own AI tools are welcome and appear in the report, but are not scored here.
